Want to check outgoing links on a website for either unsafe links, or where the content is not related to geograph.org.uk as site a user-collected photos sharing site of locations in Britain and Ireland

Also want to know where the URL does NOT match the content. Eg the URL is clearly says something like crawleytownhall.co.uk, but the content is all about casinos (the domain has probably been taken over and redirected)

Definitions:
normal: Highly relevant and safe. Site content directly relates to the geography, history, culture, or natural environment of a specific location in Britain and Ireland. It appears to be a legitimate, valuable resource.
personal: Safe, non-commercial personal content. A personal website, blog, or profile that is clearly non-commercial and focuses on personal hobbies or experiences relevant to the location.
unsafe: Content violates safeguarding policy. Leads to an adult site, illicit content, malware, or any content considered explicitly harmful or unsafe for children. Crucially, if the observed title/content is a browser security warning like 'Your connection is not private', it must be classified as unsafe.
unrelated: Irrelevant but Safe. Content is safe but has no contextual connection to UK/Ireland geography or the project's scope. This is a topic that simply does not belong, even if high-quality.
spam: Manipulative/Commercial Intent. The link's primary purpose is SEO manipulation, link dropping, or unrelated commercial promotion. This includes sites that use deceptive branding or look-alike names (e.g., abusing Amazon's name) to promote a service. Content is typically low-quality, generic, or an obvious pretext for marketing.
mismatch: URL and content are divergent (domain takeover). The content displayed is fundamentally different from what the URL suggests, indicating the domain has been repurposed or acquired.
gone: Permanently Deleted (Hard Error). The site returns a definitive, explicit HTTP 404 (Not Found) or 410 (Gone) status code, or a server-generated page clearly indicating the resource is permanently unavailable.
holding: Inactive Placeholder. The URL resolves to a static, low-value page (e.g., a "Coming Soon," domain parking, or registrar template) that contains no active, informative content.
unknown: Inaccessible/Ambiguous Data. Classification is impossible due to temporary connection issues (e.g., timeout, CAPTCHA, blocked or connection refused) or a transient soft 404 that is not a definitive 404 error.
other: Residual category. Anything else that does not fit the above categories.
